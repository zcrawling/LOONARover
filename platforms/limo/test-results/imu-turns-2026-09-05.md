# Stationary gyro and small-angle turns

Read-only analysis of the new stationary and left/right turn bags on LIMO.
No sensor gains, covariance, track width, or control parameters were changed.
The user explicitly reports large angle-measurement uncertainty and a visually
good return to the original heading after left then right rotation.

## Stationary record

Trial `20260905_161345_653130_stationary_90s_01`, requested 90 seconds,
approximately 92.003 seconds of recorded IMU data including recording margins:

- 9,192 IMU messages.
- Mean wz +0.000130444 rad/s, approximately +0.007474 degrees/s.
- Sample variance 1.11813e-6 (rad/s)^2, standard deviation 0.001057 rad/s.
- Gyro integrated rotation +0.68826 degrees; EKF +0.68837 degrees.
- MCU orientation yaw change 0 degrees; encoder travel and EKF translation 0 m.
- Ten-second mean wz ranges +0.0000891 to +0.0002009 rad/s.
- Maximum IMU timestamp interval 12.03 ms.

This is evidence of a small positive gyro bias in this stationary session,
equivalent to about 0.448 degrees/minute. It is not yet evidence of temperature
or restart stability. Subtracting a fitted bias on the same recording would
not constitute independent validation. Raw stationary variance also should not
automatically replace operational covariance for vibrating/moving conditions.

The two recorded gyro angle changes sum to -0.3181 degrees; filtered odometry
sums to -0.3195 degrees. This supports good left/right symmetry in these two
trials. The bags are separated by minutes, so their sum is not continuous
odometry closure across the unrecorded interval. MCU orientation is an onboard
estimate, not independent angle ground truth.

Do not fit a 28/26 angle gain from the approximate manual notes. The old raw
wheel heading remains inaccurate and asymmetric; these uncertain angle samples
are also insufficient to calibrate effective track width for skid-steer turns.
Using encoder translation and IMU rotation in final odometry is supported by
these recordings. Current distance and angular gains are retained.
