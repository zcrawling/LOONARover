# Timed-motion recorder correction

The four original bags were copied from LIMO to `data/limo/odom_tests/` without changing their measurements. Every bag contains a zero command followed by additional nonzero commands, with no final zero after the last nonzero command. The old shell implementation sent zero before terminating its periodic publisher and timed execution from subprocess creation rather than first publication.

The runner now uses one rclpy publisher and a monotonic duration measured at the start of publication. Normal expiry and SIGINT/SIGTERM finish with a zero command from that same publisher; the recorder retains a further second before finalizing. Actual publication timestamps and requested commands are saved to `command-events.jsonl`. `trial.yaml` preserves status and elapsed command time even after interruption; manual fields are distance and heading error. Recorder subscription is confirmed before publication. Requested speed values are not scaled or clamped.

## Verification

Integration tests ran on LIMO in a loopback-only, separate ROS domain 167, without rover subscribers. Recorded `/cmd_vel` samples were deserialized and checked, including final stop order and finalized bag metadata.

- Requested 2 s: observed first motion to zero 2.000265162 s, 40 total samples, no motion after stop.
- SIGINT during motion: observed first motion to zero 0.402051169 s, 10 total samples, no motion after stop; status `interrupted`.

Use `platforms/limo/tools/verify_odom_motion_test.py` under sourced ROS Humble to reproduce the isolated checks. The operational shell command remains unchanged and loads the Python implementation beside it.
